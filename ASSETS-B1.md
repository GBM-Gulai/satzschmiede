# Satzschmiede – B1 art list 🎨 (today's Vienna)

Everything the new level **B1 "Der Job"** still needs – **34 images**. Until an image exists, the game shows an emoji, so you can make them in any order.
The ☐ column is for ticking off what you've done.

## How to make them

1. Open Gemini and start a **new chat** for B1.
2. Send the **style message** below first, then the prompts one at a time.
3. If an image drifts in style: *"Same style as before, please – painterly storybook, warm light, modern Vienna."*
4. Save as **JPG** with the **exact lowercase file name** in the folder shown (Gemini's long names are fine too – tell me and I'll rename them).
5. Squares (1:1) for characters, enemies and encounters · tall 9:16 for backgrounds · tall 2:3 for card frames.

## Style message (send this first)

> I'm making a card game called "Satzschmiede" about a newcomer learning German in Vienna. This chapter happens in **today's Vienna**: job centre, office, presentations. Please create all images in ONE consistent style: **painterly digital illustration in a whimsical European storybook style**, visible soft brush strokes, warm light, muted palette of deep teal, moss green, amber and burnt orange, gentle paper grain, slightly magical and humorous. Everything is **modern** – screens, phones, office furniture, modern clothes – but the monsters are everyday office things that have come alive. Characters are centred, shown from the waist or chest up, with a simple softly blurred background. No text, no letters, no logos, no frames, no watermarks. Reply "ready" and then I'll send the images one by one.
